# June AI Receptionist

June is an AI receptionist for home-services businesses. It answers the business phone line live, 24/7, in the business's own name.

Website: https://answerjune.com

## What June does

- Answers incoming calls to the business phone number, live, around the clock
- Speaks in the business's name and tone
- Answers configured questions about the business (hours, service areas, services offered)
- Books jobs and captures caller details (name, address, issue description)
- Logs every call with details so the owner can review when free

## What June refuses to do

These refusals are hard constraints, not configurable:

- Never quotes a price or cost estimate to a caller
- Never promises an arrival time or scheduling window it cannot verify
- Never claims to be human
- Never provides phone-based diagnosis or technical advice
- Never makes commitments the business has not explicitly authorized
- Never shares the business owner's personal contact information with callers

## Target market

Home-services businesses: plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians, cleaners, roofers, landscapers, pest control, and similar trade professionals.
